Kaal Clock

Task Alarm & Focus. An alarm that stops only after you are really awake, a calm focus timer, and a beautiful 30-muhurat watch from the Indian tradition, with a normal 24-hour clock too.

Get it on Google PlayPrivacy Policy
⏰  Task alarm

Turn the alarm off only after walking steps, squats, sums, shaking the phone or typing a line. Combine several tasks.

🔁  Hard to skip

It rings over the lock screen, keeps your progress, and rings again if the phone is restarted.

🎯  Focus timer

Count up or down, digital or analog, full screen, with rain, fire or wind-chime sounds and a daily streak.

🕰️  Three clocks

30 muhurat with ghati·pal or kala·kashtha, or a normal 24-hour clock. Home-screen widgets in light and dark.

🍲  Daily food & routine

Seasonal food and daily-routine traditions from the classical texts, for your place and weather.

🔒  Private by design

No account, no servers. Alarms, settings and history stay on your phone. 11 languages.
