All features

⏰ Alarms that make sure you are awake

  • Add alarm in one simple screen: time, name, ringtone and tasks.
  • Set the time in muhurat and kala, or type a normal clock time; it is shown big in AM/PM.
  • Turn-off tasks: tap, solve sums, walk steps, squats (up and down), shake the phone, or type a shloka line. Choose how many (for example 80 steps).
  • Combine tasks: up to 4 in a row, for example walk 50 steps, then solve 3 sums.
  • Any ringtone: singing bowl, temple bell, muhurat bell, soft ding, the phone's alarm tone or any song on your phone, with a ▶ preview.
  • Repeat on chosen days, and 🔔 Test this alarm to try it right away.

🔔 When it rings

  • Rings over the lock screen and grows louder slowly, with vibration.
  • Back does not close it; if you press Home, the alarm comes back (optional permission).
  • Progress is kept: leave and return, and your steps or sums continue where you were.
  • If the phone is switched off while ringing, the alarm rings again after the restart.
  • Snooze 5 kala (8 minutes) for simple tap alarms.

🎯 Focus timer

  • Default 1 ghati (24 minutes) focus and 3 kala break; change focus, break, long break and rounds with − and +.
  • Count down or up; show time in minutes or ghati · pal; digital or analog dial.
  • Full screen: a big clean timer, tap to pause, turn sideways, Back to leave.
  • Soft sounds made in the app: rain, fire, wind chimes, ocean waves, soft wind, tick.
  • History of the last 14 days, weekly total and streak, saved on your phone.

🕰️ Three ways to read time

  • 30 muhurat of 48 minutes from midnight, read as ghati · pal · vipal or kala · kashtha.
  • A normal 24-hour clock for anyone who prefers usual time.
  • A brass-and-indigo watch face with day, night and dosha rings; light and dark themes.
  • Time zone follows your place, your phone, India (IST) or any zone you pick.

📱 Home-screen widgets

  • Four widgets: full, digital, analog and a Nothing-style dot design.
  • Light, dark or automatic style, chosen in Settings.

🍲 Aaj: today's food and routine

  • Seasonal food and daily-routine traditions from the classical texts, for your place and season, changing every day.
  • Optional weather (MET Norway) and air quality for India (CPCB) to match today's temperature.
  • Five new lines every day: food, kitchen herbs and spices, habit, exercise and a shloka.
  • Covers every Indian district and 1,500+ cities worldwide; seasons flip south of the equator.

📚 Gyaan

  • The 30-muhurat guide, the classical daily order (dinacharya), the dosha clock and a season-by-season food guide.
  • Sources named for every point (Charaka Samhita, Ashtanga Hridaya and more).

🤖 Optional AI guide

  • With your own free Gemini key (or a Claude key), get a daily note and answers about food and routine for your place.
  • Find working models tests which models your key can use. The key is encrypted on your phone.

🌐 Languages and more

  • English, Hinglish, हिन्दी, বাংলা, मराठी, தமிழ், తెలుగు, Español, Français, Deutsch, Português.
  • A short guide on first launch; reopen it any time in Settings → About.
  • A clear Save button in Settings, and Delete all my data in one tap.

🔒 Privacy, ads and safety

  • No account and no servers. Alarms, settings and history never leave your phone. Read the Privacy Policy.
  • Free with Google ads: a small banner, an optional 24-hour unlock video, and at most one short break ad a day. The clock, alarms and widgets are always free.
  • Food and routine traditions only, never medical advice. Read the Health Disclaimer.
  • Delete everything any time. See Delete your data.
