Health Disclaimer

Effective date: 23 November 2026

Kaal Clock is a time-keeping app with cultural, food and daily-routine knowledge from the classical Indian texts. It does not give health, medical or nutrition advice and it is not a medical device.

  • Foods, kitchen herbs and spices are named only as food, together with what the old texts traditionally say about seasons, taste and daily routine. Nothing in the app says or means that any food, herb, spice or habit prevents, treats, cures or improves any disease or condition.
  • Foods and herbs are not suitable for everyone. They can cause allergies or interact with medicines, and may be unsuitable in pregnancy, while breastfeeding, for children or older people, and for people with diabetes, blood-pressure, kidney, liver, heart, thyroid or other conditions. Check with a qualified doctor before changing your diet, sleep or exercise.
  • Do not start, stop or change any prescribed medicine or treatment because of anything in this app.
  • Exercise, walking and squat tasks in alarms are optional and should be done only if they are safe for you.
  • Text written by the optional AI guide is generated by a third-party AI and can be wrong or incomplete. It is not reviewed by a doctor.
  • Ads in the app are chosen and served by Google. We do not endorse, check or recommend any product, medicine, treatment or service shown in an ad.
  • In an emergency, call your local emergency number (112 in India) or go to the nearest hospital.

By using the app you accept that you use its information at your own discretion and risk.
