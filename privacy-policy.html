Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Kaal Clock ("the app") is built so that your information stays on your phone. We, the developer, do not run any server, do not ask you to make an account, and do not ourselves collect, store or sell your personal data. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob (a small banner, and a short video ad you can choose to watch once a day to unlock the Focus timer and AI vaidya), and AdMob collects some device data as explained under Advertising.

What stays on your phone

Your settings, alarms, focus history, chosen city, cached daily notes and, if you add one, your AI API key are saved only in the app's private storage on your device. Nobody else, including us, can see them. They are deleted when you tap Settings → Delete all my data or uninstall the app.

What the app does not do

  • No account, sign-in or analytics. The only third-party SDK is Google AdMob, for ads.
  • No access to your GPS location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera.
  • We do not sell your data, and we never receive your settings, alarms, history or questions.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows small banner ads from Google AdMob, only on the Aaj page below the watch and on the Gyaan page. Once a day you may choose to watch one short video ad (a "rewarded" ad) to unlock the Focus timer and AI vaidya until midnight; it is never shown without your tap, and if no ad is available the features unlock anyway. Ads never appear on the clock, alarms or home-screen widgets.

To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects and sends to Google: device identifiers (such as the Android advertising ID), IP address (which gives an approximate location), app interactions with ads, ad performance, and diagnostics such as crash data. This data is encrypted in transit and is handled by Google under its own Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services. We do not receive this data in a form that identifies you.

  • Your choices. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form is shown before ads load. Anyone can change ad consent later in Settings → Ad privacy choices (where available).
  • Advertising ID. You can reset or delete it in Android Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Ad content is limited to a general-audience rating. We do not choose individual ads and do not endorse any product advertised.

Optional online features (off until you turn them on)

Apart from ads, the clock, alarms, focus timer and all built-in knowledge work fully offline. These features use the internet only if you switch them on in Settings:

  • Weather and air quality. The app sends the latitude and longitude of the place you picked (not your GPS position) to MET Norway (api.met.no) for weather, and, for places in India, the state name to the Government of India's open data platform (data.gov.in, CPCB air-quality data) for air quality. Their own privacy policies apply. Weather data © MET Norway, CC BY 4.0; air data under the Government Open Data Licence – India.
  • AI vaidya. If you add your own Google Gemini or Anthropic Claude API key, the app sends your question and today's context (date, city, season, weather) directly from your phone to that provider. The provider's terms and privacy policy apply. We never see your key or your questions.

Permissions and why

  • Notifications, exact alarms (alarm clock), full-screen alarm, vibration, run at start-up: to ring your alarms, chimes and focus timer on time, even when the phone is locked or restarted.
  • Foreground service (media playback): to keep the alarm sound playing until you complete the turn-off task.
  • Internet, network state and advertising ID: for Google AdMob ads and the optional features above.
  • Display over other apps (optional, you turn it on from the Alarm page): used only while an alarm is ringing, to bring the alarm screen back if you press Home or open another app before finishing the task. The app does not read, record or control what is on your screen or in other apps, and it shows nothing over other apps at any other time. You can switch it off any time in Android Settings → Apps → Kaal Clock → Display over other apps.
  • Motion sensor (accelerometer, no permission prompt): only during an alarm's walk, squat or shake task, to count the movement.
  • Choose a ringtone: the phone's own sound picker; the app stores only the link to the sound you chose.
