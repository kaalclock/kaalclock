Privacy Policy

Effective date: 23 November 2026 · Applies to the Kaal Clock Android app (package app.muhurat.clock), version 2.9 and later.

Kaal Clock ("the app", "we", "us") is an alarm clock, focus timer and 30-muhurat watch made by an independent developer in India. This policy explains, in plain words, what information the app uses, where it goes, and the choices you have. The short version: we do not run any server, we do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect, store, sell or share your personal data ourselves. Your alarms, settings and history stay on your phone. The free app shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google collects some device data for those ads, as explained below.

1. Information that stays only on your phone

The app saves the following in its private storage on your device. We cannot see it, and it is never sent to us:

  • Your settings: language, theme, units, time zone, chosen city or district, widget style and similar choices.
  • Your alarms: times, names, repeat days, chosen ringtone (only a link to the sound on your phone), turn-off tasks and counts.
  • Your focus timer settings and focus history (how many minutes you focused on each day).
  • Cached daily notes, today's weather and air-quality figures if you turned weather on.
  • Technical records the app needs to work: the time the 24-hour unlock started, the time a break ad was last shown, and, only while an alarm is ringing, the alarm's name and tasks so that it can ring again if the phone is restarted before the task is done. This record is removed as soon as the alarm is turned off.
  • Your AI API key, if you add one. It is encrypted with the Android Keystore, never shown back on screen and never sent to us.

This information is excluded from Android cloud backups. It stays until you change it, tap Settings → Delete all my data, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app; any of these deletes it permanently.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and is paid for by ads from Google AdMob. Three kinds of ads may appear:

  • Banner: a small strip at the bottom of the Aaj page (below the watch) and of the Gyaan page.
  • Unlock video (rewarded ad): shown only when you tap Watch ad and unlock. After it, Gyaan, the focus timer, full-screen focus and the AI guide stay open for 24 hours.
  • Break ad (interstitial): at most once in 24 hours and only right after you finish something, such as a focus session or saving an alarm. It is never shown when the app opens, while an alarm rings, on your first day, or on a day you watched the unlock video.

Ads never appear on the watch face, the ringing alarm screen or home-screen widgets, and the clock, alarms and widgets are never locked behind an ad.

To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud and abuse, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects and sends to Google: the Android advertising ID and other device identifiers, IP address (from which an approximate location can be inferred), information about your interactions with ads and the app, ad performance, and crash and diagnostic information. The data is encrypted in transit. Google handles it as described in the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. We do not receive this information in a form that identifies you.

  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland and other places where the law requires it): Google's consent form is shown before personalised ads load. You can change your choice any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete it in Android Settings → Google → Ads (on some phones Settings → Privacy → Ads). With a deleted ID, ads are not personalised.
  • Ad content is limited to a general-audience rating. We do not choose individual ads and do not endorse any product, service, medicine or treatment shown in an ad.

4. Optional online features

Apart from ads and update checks, the clock, alarms, focus timer, widgets and all built-in knowledge work fully offline. These features use the internet only if you turn them on:

  • Weather and air quality (Settings → Show weather and air quality, off by default). The app sends the latitude and longitude of the place you picked, never your GPS position, to MET Norway (api.met.no), and for places in India the state name to data.gov.in for CPCB air-quality data. Their own privacy policies apply. Weather data © MET Norway, licensed CC BY 4.0; air-quality data under the Government Open Data Licence – India.
  • AI guide (Settings → AI guide, off until you add a key). The app sends your question and today's context (date, place, season, weather) straight from your phone to Google or Anthropic using your own API key. Their terms and privacy policies apply, and any charges on your key are between you and them. We never see your key, your questions or the answers.

5. Permissions and why the app asks for them

  • Notifications: to show alarms, the focus timer and the optional morning note.
  • Alarms and reminders (exact alarms): Kaal Clock is an alarm clock; your alarms, snooze, the focus timer and the optional muhurat chime must ring at the exact time.
  • Full-screen alarm: to show the ringing alarm over the lock screen.
  • Foreground service (media playback): to keep the alarm sound playing until you finish the turn-off task.
  • Run at start-up: to set your alarms again after the phone restarts, and to ring an alarm again if the phone was switched off while it was ringing.
  • Vibration and wake lock: to vibrate and keep the alarm going while the screen is off.
  • Display over other apps (optional): you turn it on from the Alarm page. It is used only while an alarm is ringing, to bring the alarm screen back if you press Home or open another app before finishing the task. The app never reads, records or controls your screen or other apps, and shows nothing over other apps at any other time. You can turn it off in Android Settings → Apps → Kaal Clock → Display over other apps.
  • Internet, network state and advertising ID: for ads, update checks and the optional features above.
  • Motion sensor (no permission prompt): only during an alarm's walk, squat or shake task, to count the movement.

The app does not request location, contacts, camera, microphone, storage, phone, SMS or call-log permissions.

6. How we protect information

  • All network connections use encrypted HTTPS; plain HTTP is turned off.
  • Your AI key is encrypted with a key held in the Android Keystore.
  • App data lives in Android's private app storage, which other apps cannot read, and is not included in cloud backups.
  • The app's screens are built into the app; it does not load web pages from the internet, and links open in your browser.
  • We keep no copy of your data on any server, so there is nothing of yours for us to lose or leak.

7. Your rights and choices

Because your information stays on your device, you are in full control of it:

  • See and change your data in the app at any time.
  • Delete it with Settings → Delete all my data, by clearing the app's storage, or by uninstalling.
  • Withdraw consent for ads in Settings → Ad privacy choices; turn off weather or remove your AI key in Settings.
  • For data held by Google, MET Norway, data.gov.in, Google Gemini or Anthropic, use their own privacy tools or contact them; we cannot access it.

These choices apply to everyone, including users protected by the EU/UK GDPR, the California Consumer Privacy Act and India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising ourselves; AdMob's processing is described in section 3.

8. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and should not be used by them. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has used the app, uninstalling it removes all of its data from the device; you can also contact us.

9. Sharing, sale and legal requests

We do not sell, rent or trade personal information. Since we do not hold your data, we have nothing to hand over in a sale of the app or in response to a legal request. Any such request about ad data would be a matter for Google.

10. International users

The developer is in India. When you use ads, weather or the AI guide, your data goes directly from your phone to those providers, which may process it in other countries under their own safeguards.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will appear in the app (Settings → Legal) and on this page with a new effective date. Important changes will be noted in the app's release notes.
