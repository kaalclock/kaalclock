Delete your data

App: Kaal Clock (Google Play package app.muhurat.clock) · Developer contact: support.kaalclock@gmail.com

Kaal Clock has no accounts and the developer keeps no copy of your data on any server. Everything the app saves is stored only on your phone, so you can delete it yourself at any time, instantly.

Option 1: inside the app

  1. Open Kaal Clock.
  2. Go to Settings (bottom right).
  3. Scroll to Privacy and online features.
  4. Tap Delete all my data, then tap it again to confirm.

Option 2: from Android settings

  1. Open Android Settings → Apps → Kaal Clock → Storage.
  2. Tap Clear storage (or Clear data).

Option 3: uninstall the app

Uninstalling Kaal Clock removes all of its data from your phone.

What is deleted

  • Alarms, their tasks and ringtones choices
  • Focus timer settings and focus history
  • Settings: language, place, time zone, units, theme, widget style
  • Your AI API key (encrypted) and cached daily notes
  • Unlock and ad timing records

Deletion is immediate and permanent. Nothing is kept by the developer, and there is no retention period, because we never receive this data.

Data held by Google for ads

Ads are served by Google AdMob, which receives device identifiers, IP address and ad interaction data. To reset or delete your advertising ID, open Android Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads). To manage other data held by Google, visit Google Data & privacy. Data sent to MET Norway, data.gov.in, Google Gemini or Anthropic (only if you turned on weather or the AI guide) is handled under their own policies.
